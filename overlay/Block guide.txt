Script Playground: block guide
=============================

Block scripts are built from blocks, like Scratch. Click New, then "Block script".
Basic and Advanced (top right) switch between blocks and the same script as JavaScript.
Drag a block from the left onto the canvas, or click it to add it at the end.
Click a block to choose what it works on in the panel on the right.

The panel on the right
  - Each slot of the block has a button at the top (for example "Item", "On", "How many").
    Click one, then pick from the list below it, or type a name and press Enter.
  - The lists come from the game itself: every tree, rock, fish, recipe, spell, prayer,
    creature, seed, familiar and piece of furniture, with the level it needs.
  - "Near you" lists show what is around your character right now.
  - "What it needs" at the bottom checks your levels, the items to bring and any quest.
    A tick means you have the level, a cross means you don't yet.

Finding blocks
  The buttons above the blocks are the tabs: Basics, Bank & Items, Use & Make, Gathering,
  Hunter & Farming, Combat & Magic, House, Logic and Conditions.
  Type in "Search blocks" to search every tab at once.

When a block can't do its job
  The script stops, the block turns red, and the game chat says why in plain words,
  for example "you need Woodcutting level 15 to cut Oak. Yours is 1." or
  "you must complete the Druidic Ritual quest before you can make Guam potion(unf)."

Basics
  Walk To          a place from the list, or anyone from the People and Monsters lists, even far
                   away (it goes to where they live, then up to them). Plans the whole route,
                   opens doors and gates, crosses stiles and toll gates (bring 10 coins for
                   Al Kharid), and takes stairs, ladders and trapdoors to other floors.
                   Agility shortcuts (like the crumbling wall in Falador) are used only when your
                   Agility level is high enough; otherwise it walks the long way round.
                   Places you can only reach by boat, by a quest or by teleport aren't in the list.
                   Some people and monsters live where you can't walk (deep dungeons, islands,
                   minigames): Walk To says so. Teleport near them first, then Walk To works.
                   Or map coordinates, like 3222, 3218 (add a third number for the floor:
                   3205, 3209, 1). Type them in the boxes, press Where I am, or press Grab from game
                   and click a tile in the game. Tools > Grab Coordinates copies a tile's coordinates.
  Click Object     any option on the nearest object: Open a door, Climb-up a ladder, Search a crate.
  Click Person     any option on the nearest person or creature: Trade, Pickpocket, Talk-to.
  Talk To          talks to someone and waits for them to answer.
  Press Key        Space or Enter continues a conversation, 1 to 5 picks an option, Escape closes.
                   List several keys with commas: "Space, Space, 2".
  Wait For         waits until a dialogue, options, a "how many" menu or a typing box shows up,
                   or until the dialogue has closed ("No dialogue").
  Type Answer      types into an "Enter amount" box (or a name box) and presses Enter.
  Say              says something in the game chat.
  Run              turns running on or off. Running uses run energy, which comes back as you walk.
                   Auto turns it on now and again every time your energy gets back up to the %
                   you set (0 to 100; full when left empty), until the script ends or a Run Off.
                   Put it first in the script so it covers every walk.

Bank & Items
  Open Bank, Deposit, Withdraw, Close Bank
                   Deposit takes all items, some inventory slots (like 2-28) or one kind of item.
                   Withdraw 0 means all of them.
  Pick Up          picks up an item from the ground nearby.
  Drop             drops one kind of item, or some inventory slots.
  Eat              eats a food, or "Any food".
  Bury Bones       buries all bones of a kind, or "Any bones".

Use & Make
  Use Item on Item     uses one item on another (a herb on a vial of water, a knife on logs).
                       If a "how many" menu opens it picks the product and amount for you.
                       Leave "Make" empty to take the first product.
  Use Slot on Slot     the same, by inventory slot number (1 to 28).
  Use Item on Target   uses an item on something nearby: a range, a furnace, an altar, a person,
                       a farming patch or an item on the ground.
  Use Slot on Target   the same, by inventory slot number.
  Make Item            names the product and the game works out how: a recipe, or the furnace,
                       anvil, spinning wheel, tanner or obelisk menu. Stand next to the station.
  Choose in Menu       picks a product in a "how many" menu that is already open.
  Light Fire           lights logs with a tinderbox and steps aside if a fire can't go there.

Gathering
  Chop Tree, Mine Rock, Fish
                   checks your level and your axe, pickaxe, net, rod or bait first.
                   Fish also finds the spots: name a fish (Lobster, Trout, Shrimps...) and if no
                   spot for it is nearby, it walks to the nearest one you can reach on foot.
                   It skips the Wilderness, islands, and the Fishing Guild until Fishing 68.
                   Walk To takes a fish name too (Shrimps, Lobster, Shark...): it goes to the
                   spot for that fish that takes the fewest steps to walk to.
  Craft Runes      crafts essence at an altar, entering the ruins with the talisman or tiara.
                   Needs the Rune Mysteries quest (and others for some altars).
  Steal            pickpockets a person, steals from a stall or searches a chest.
                   Waits out a stun if you get caught. Eat first if your HP is low.
  Run Agility Course
                   goes round the Gnome Stronghold, Barbarian Outpost, Wilderness course or the
                   Agility Pyramid for as many laps as you choose. On the pyramid it climbs to
                   the top each lap and takes the pyramid top, and after a slip it carries on
                   from the floor you land on. Bring waterskins for the desert heat; it stops
                   when your HP gets low. Brimhaven arena needs Click Object for each obstacle.

Hunter & Farming
  Hunt             lays as many traps as your Hunter level allows for a creature, waits for
                   a catch and collects it. Carry the traps (bird snares, box traps...).
  Lay Trap, Check Traps
                   one trap at a time, and collecting what was caught.
  Track Kebbit     follows the tracks from a burrow. Needs a noose wand.
  Rake Patch, Compost Patch, Plant, Water Patch, Cure Patch, Harvest
                   work on the nearest patch of that kind ("Herb patch", "Allotment"),
                   or the nearest patch of any kind ("Farming patch").
                   Plant uses a seed dibber for seeds and a spade for saplings.
                   Compost goes on an empty, weeded patch before planting.

Combat & Magic
  Attack           fights the nearest monster with that name until it dies.
  Attack Slayer Task
                   fights the nearest monster of your current Slayer task.
  Auto Retaliate   on or off.
  Cast Spell       teleports (nothing to choose), alchemy and enchanting (choose an item),
                   combat spells (choose a monster). Checks your level, runes and spellbook.
                   An elemental staff counts as its runes.
  Prayer           turns a prayer on or off.
  Recharge         prays at the nearest altar, or renews Summoning points at an obelisk.
  Summon           summons a familiar from its pouch. Needs the Wolf Whistle quest.
                   Make pouches with Make Item at an obelisk.

House
  Enter House      goes in through your house portal, normally or in building mode.
  Build            builds furniture on the nearest empty spot that takes it.
                   Needs building mode, a hammer, a saw and the materials.

Logic
  Repeat Forever, Repeat (a number of times), Repeat Until (a condition),
  If, If / Else, Wait (seconds), Stop Script.
  JavaScript       runs your own code. Click it to edit the code in the panel on the right.
                   Code from the Advanced view that isn't a block shows up as this block.

Conditions (for If and Repeat Until)
  Inventory Is Full / Is Not Full / Is Empty, Has Item, Does Not Have Item,
  HP Below %, Is In Combat, Is Not In Combat, Is At (a place or map coordinates), Bank Is Open,
  Dialogue Is Open, Options Are Showing, Make Menu Is Open, Quest Is Complete,
  Level Is At Least, Level Is, Level Is Not, Level Is Below (each with a skill and a level,
  like "If Woodcutting Level Is Below 15"), Has Slayer Task, Prayer Is On,
  Prayer Points Below, Has Familiar, Is In House, Run Is On, Run Is Off,
  Run Energy Below and Run Energy Is At Least (a % from 1 to 100).

Examples
  The scripts folder has an example for each kind of skill, named example_....blocks:
  chopping and banking, fishing and cooking, smelting, potions, talking to Hans,
  pickpocketing, agility laps, High Alchemy, a herb run, hunting, runecrafting,
  Slayer, prayer, Summoning and building in your house.
  Open one, click Run, and watch each block light up as it runs.

Blocks in JavaScript
  Every block is also a function you can call from a JavaScript script, inside blocks.run:

    blocks.run(async () => {
      await blocks.walk_to("b1", "Draynor Willows");
      await blocks.fish("b2", "Shrimps");
      await blocks.use_item_on_item("b3", "Knife", "Logs", 0, "Arrow shaft");
    });

  The first value is a name for the step (it shows in the log when something stops).
